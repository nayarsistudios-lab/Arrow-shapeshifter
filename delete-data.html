Arrow Shapeshifter · Nayarsi Studios

What the game stores

Arrow Shapeshifter does not ask you to create an account, and Nayarsi Studios does not run its own servers. Your game data is kept in these places:

  • On your phone: level progress, stars, tokens, unlocked themes, collection and settings.
  • Google Play Games cloud save (only if you are signed in): a backup copy of the same progress, stored in your Google account.
  • Google services used by the game: Google Play (purchases), Google AdMob (ads) and anonymous gameplay statistics.

1. Delete the data on your phone

Choose one:

  1. Uninstall Arrow Shapeshifter, or
  2. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Arrow Shapeshifter → Storage and tap Clear data.

This removes all progress stored on the phone. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the cloud copy is not removed by this step (see below).

2. Delete your cloud save

Your cloud save is stored by Google Play Games in your Google account. To delete it:

  1. Open the Google Play Games settings on your phone (in the Play Games app, or in the Play Store under your profile).
  2. Find the option to delete your Play Games data and choose Arrow Shapeshifter.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

The exact menu names can differ between phones and Play Games versions. You can also manage or delete your Google account data at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

3. Advertising and statistics

  • To reset or delete your advertising ID, open your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics are linked only to this installation of the game. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data (step 1) starts a new, unconnected record.

Purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads and themes) are handled and recorded by Google Play, not by Nayarsi Studios. Deleting your game data does not cancel or remove a purchase: after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases in the game to get them back. For purchase records or refunds, see your Google Play order history.

Need help?

If you have any question about your data, or want us to help with a deletion request, email Nayarsistudios@gmail.com with the subject "Arrow Shapeshifter data deletion". We reply within 30 days.
